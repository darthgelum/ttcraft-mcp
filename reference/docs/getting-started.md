## What is TTCraft

TTCraft (TableTopCraft) is a platform for tabletop RPG and board-game communities.
Life on TTCraft happens in **rooms**: each room belongs to a community and holds its
conversations, its games and its people. Inside rooms you will find **channels** for
talking and **game tables** for playing; next to all of that live **modules** — game
content published by other users — and **direct messages** for private conversations.

## Create an account

TTCraft uses one-time codes. To create an account by email:

1. Open **Sign up**. Choose a username, enter your email and birth date,
   review and accept the terms, then click **Continue**. Display name is optional.
2. Enter the six-digit code sent to your email and click **Confirm**.

To sign in later, enter your email or username. If you connected an authenticator
app under **Settings → Security**, enter its current code or use a saved recovery
code. The sign-in screen also lets you request an email code. Email codes expire
after 10 minutes. If a message does not arrive, check your spam folder and request
a new code when the resend timer ends.

If **Google** or **Discord** is available, you can use it to sign up or sign in.
To connect a provider to an existing account, sign in first and link it in
**Settings → Security**. You can also unlink it there and continue signing in
by email.

## Rooms and channels

A **room** is a community's space: a game group, a club, a circle of friends. Every
room is organised into **channels**, and a channel comes in one of two kinds:

- **Text channels** — ordinary live chat: messages, replies, reactions.
- **Post channels** — long-form posts for announcements, session recaps and
  campaign notes, with comments under each post.

### Game tables

A **game table** opens from a text channel. A member with **Manage table**
can use **Open table** to start it. Other members with channel access can use
**Join table** to play in their browser. The chat remains available in the channel
while the table is open. A table manager can close it after the session.

You do not need a group to try one: the [practice table](/table/tutorial) is
yours alone, and it walks you through picking things up, rolling dice and dealing
cards before you sit down with anyone.

Tables are programmable: any object on a table can carry a Lua script, and the
table itself has a Global script for house rules and custom UI — see
[Table scripting](/docs/scripting), or edit the scripts from VS Code with the
[TTCraft VSC extension](/docs/vscode). A table can be saved, downloaded and loaded
as a [table archive](/docs/table-archives).

## Modules

**Modules** are game content published by TTCraft users: adventures, rule variants,
material for your table. Browse the module catalogue, open any module to read it,
and publish your own when you have something to share with other game masters and
players.

## Direct messages

Not everything belongs in a room. **Direct messages** are private one-on-one
conversations — write directly to anyone you play with.

## Quick reference

| Term | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Room | A community space that holds channels, tables and members |
| Channel | A text chat or a post feed inside a room |
| Game table | A shared play space opened from a text channel |
| Module | Game content published by a user |
| Direct message | A private conversation between two users |
